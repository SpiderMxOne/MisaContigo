Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Diego Torres Barragán ("we") operates the MisaContigo mobile application. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your spiritual and personal data.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

Our app is designed under the principle of privacy by design. Data is handled as follows:

  • Family Circle & Intentions: Names of family/friends and shared intentions are securely stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) to enable real-time synchronization and collaborative management (unique user prayer count and intention deletion).
  • Android Auto & Audio Playback: Android Auto and voice audio playback utilize local device speech synthesis (`TextToSpeech`) and Media3 locally, without recording or transmitting voice recordings to external servers.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Spiritual reflection requests are processed anonymously through the Google Gemini API.
  • Financial Information: MisaContigo uses the official Google Play billing system for offerings. We do not collect or store your credit card or payment method details; that information is managed exclusively by Google.
  • Authentication: We use Firebase anonymous authentication to securely identify your participation in family circles without requiring personal passwords.

2. Local Storage

Your theme preferences, selected country, and favorite prayers are saved locally on your device using DataStore.

3. Third Parties & Services

We use trusted Google services:

  • Firebase: Family Circle and intention synchronization.
  • Gemini API: Generation of reflections and catechesis.
  • Google Play Billing: Secure processing of offerings.

4. Security & Contact

We implement technical measures to protect your data using secure communication protocols (HTTPS). For any inquiries: spidermxone@gmail.com
